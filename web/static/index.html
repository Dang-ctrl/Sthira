Memory for what you decided with AI

Your AI forgets what you decided. Sthira doesn’t.

It reads your ChatGPT, Claude and Claude Code history and meeting notes, keeps every decision with the exact words you used, and warns when new work goes against one.

    Reads your AI history. Exports, coding sessions, transcripts.

  3. 03
    Speaks up. Cites where you decided, before an agent undoes it.
  4. 04
    Learns from you. Your corrections become rules for the next run.
